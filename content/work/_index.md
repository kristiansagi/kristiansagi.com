#### _Where I've worked and what we built._

---

### Entringo
#### Chief Executive Officer · 2026

Entringo is the smart-parking and vehicle-flow technology company within Hansab Group, a Baltic tech group backed by private equity fund BaltCap. It designs and builds ANPR-based ticketless parking systems in Estonia for airports, hospitals, park-and-ride networks and commercial properties.

My focus: taking a Baltic-proven product international and building the foundations of a scalable export business.

**What we delivered**

+ Accelerated international growth by expanding our focus to customers and partners across Europe.
+ Built a growing network of integration partners across Western and Southern Europe, extending Entringo's reach beyond the Baltics.
+ Strengthened Entringo's position with airports, hospitals and park-and-ride operators, building a pipeline for the next stage of growth.
+ Grew the business year on year while investing in the team and capabilities needed to scale.
+ Aligned strategy and priorities with group leadership.

### Dynatrace
#### Director of Engineering and Site Lead, Tallinn · 2022–2025

+ Took over the Tallinn team after Dynatrace acquired it and tripled its headcount, with a new office, a hiring process and engineering practices built from scratch.
+ Ran delivery of monitoring products built together with teams in several other countries, in line with global product and architecture direction.

### Nevercode / Codemagic
#### Co-Founder and Head of Product Strategy · 2017–2019
#### Advisory Board · 2019–2022

+ Co-founded Nevercode and created Codemagic, a CI/CD service for mobile apps built in partnership with Google's Flutter team. Developers around the world use it today.
+ Owned product strategy and led the product and engineering teams through early growth and funding.
+ Stayed on the advisory board until 2022, advising on scaling, positioning and investor relations.
+ Nevercode later took the name Codemagic and still runs today. I have since made a full exit and moved on.
